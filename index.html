Everest base camp

The Everest Base Camp (EBC) trek in Nepal is a 12–15 day, 130km round-trip journey starting with a flight to Lukla.

Annapurna base camp

The Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) trek is a popular 7–12 day Nepali journey to a 4,130m (13,550 ft) glacial basin known as the "Annapurna Sanctuary".

Langtang Himal

The primary "base camp" area and highest overnight stop on the standard Langtang Valley trek is Kyanjin Gompa, located at approximately 3,870 meters (12,696 feet).
